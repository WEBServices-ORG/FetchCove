Separately selected tools

FetchCove invokes yt-dlp, matching Python, FFmpeg and ffprobe as separate tools.
This candidate does not bundle those programs. Their distributions retain
their own notices and licenses, which can differ from a source repository's
license because of included components and build options. Consult the exact
distribution you select; do not infer a compiled tool's license from this
catalog notice.

FFmpeg project and licensing information:
https://ffmpeg.org/
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

The locally tested Homebrew FFmpeg 9.0.2 build reports GPL version 3 or later.
That observation applies to that external installation, not every FFmpeg build.
Any future plan to bundle tools requires a fresh inventory and license review
of the chosen artifacts and their components.
